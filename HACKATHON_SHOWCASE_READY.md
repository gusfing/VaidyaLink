## 🐛 Known Issues & Workarounds

### Issue: Browser Cache

**Workaround**: Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) before demo

### Issue: Microphone Permissions

**Workaround**: Allow permissions when prompted, test before demo

### Issue: Slow Internet

**Workaround**: Demo mode works offline, voice needs connection

### Issue: Language Not Transcribing

**Workaround**: Speak clearly, check language selection matches speech
