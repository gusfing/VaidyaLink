## 💡 Answering Judge Questions

### "Is this real AI or mock data?"

**Answer:**

> "Voice transcription uses real Sarvam AI - you can test it right now in any of 22 languages. The medical entity extraction is also real AI. We have demo mode for reliable testing, but the AI is 100% real. Would you like to try recording something?"

### "How does it handle multiple languages?"

**Answer:**

> "We use Sarvam AI which specializes in Indian languages. It can transcribe and understand medical terms in 22 languages - Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, and 18 more. This makes healthcare accessible to everyone in India, not just English speakers."

### "What's the business model?"

**Answer:**

> "Freemium model - basic features free for patients to drive adoption. Premium features for doctors and hospitals - AI summaries, analytics, telemedicine. Government partnerships for ABHA integration. API access for healthcare providers. We estimate ₹50-100 per doctor per month, with 1M+ doctors in India."

### "How do you ensure data privacy?"

**Answer:**

> "ABHA integration follows government standards. Privacy mode to hide sensitive info. Data encrypted in transit and at rest. HIPAA-compliant architecture ready. We never store raw audio - only transcriptions. Users control their data completely."

### "What's next?"

**Answer:**

> "Phase 1: More hospital integrations. Phase 2: Telemedicine features. Phase 3: AI-powered diagnosis suggestions. Phase 4: Prescription management and appointment scheduling. We're also exploring partnerships with state governments for rural healthcare."

### "How is this different from Google Translate?"

**Answer:**

> "Google Translate is general purpose. Sarvam AI specializes in Indian languages and understands medical context. We also extract structured medical data - symptoms, medications, conditions - which Google can't do. Plus, we integrate with ABHA and provide a complete healthcare workflow."

### "Can you show the code?"

**Answer:**

> "Absolutely! It's on GitHub: github.com/gusfing/VaidyaLink. We use Next.js, React, TypeScript, custom hooks for animations, and real API integrations. The progressive reveal hooks are reusable across the app. Would you like to see the implementation?"
